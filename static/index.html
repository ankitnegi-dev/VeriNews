How this works

  1. Find coverage Search cached or live news for the claim's key people, places, and numbers.
  2. Weigh the evidence Rank relevance and source credibility, then ask whether each article supports, refutes, or stays neutral.
  3. Refuse a guess Two relevant sources must agree before a verdict. Otherwise the result is "Not enough evidence."

Neutral coverage is no information. A single article never decides a verdict.
